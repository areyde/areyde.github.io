abstract: '<p><b>Rhubarb</b> is a tool for clustering similar MOOC solutions and selecting diverse examples. This tool first standardizes solutions that are algorithmically the same, then calculates the structure-aware edit distance between them, and then applies clustering. Finally, it selects one example from each of the largest clusters, thus ensuring their diversity.</p>'
